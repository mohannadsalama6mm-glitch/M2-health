# Phase 6C integration audit

This audit was completed before Phase 6C implementation. It classifies the existing surfaces and fixes the semantic contract for the integration work.

## Canonical and reusable

- Rust Reporting services and their native commands are the authority for operational Sales, Inventory, Purchasing, Customer, and Supplier reporting.
- Rust Finance services and their native commands are the authority for journals, expenses, account activity, trial balance, and the limited Sales financial summary.
- `CanonicalReports`, `CanonicalFinance`, and `CanonicalSales` are the native workspaces to extend.
- Existing branch, date, paging, money-minor-unit, and error contracts remain authoritative.

## Needs Phase 6C integration

- The native Dashboard route currently resolves to a sample-data Dashboard and must resolve to a canonical read-only Dashboard.
- Finance needs an explicit coverage/reconciliation result so historical operational records without Finance journals are visible rather than implied to be covered.
- Sales detail needs a read-only trace to related Finance journals, including an honest historical-unposted state.
- Reports and Finance need practical CSV export generated by Rust from the same canonical report services.
- Cross-domain exact-value tests must exercise operational Reporting, Finance, coverage, Dashboard, traceability, and export together.

## Fixture/demo only and isolated

- `Dashboard` and its sample widgets remain browser-preview fixtures.
- The older `Reports` and `Finance` feature components remain browser-preview fixtures.
- DemoContext data and preview quick actions remain outside the native canonical boundary.

## Semantic map

- Reporting gross/net Sales are operational document amounts. Finance Sales revenue/returns are posted journal-account movements. They should match only where every eligible operational event has a corresponding Finance journal.
- Reporting refunds describe posted operational refunds. Finance refund outflow describes the Finance account movement produced by represented refund events.
- Reporting stock is the current immutable Inventory ledger balance and is not date-sliced. Finance COGS is period journal activity and is unavailable for margin calculations when represented source journals have incomplete allocation cost.
- Reporting purchase metrics remain operational only. Phase 6 Finance intentionally has no supplier payables, purchase journals, or statutory accounting.
- Operating result is the implemented limited result: gross margin less recorded operating expenses. It is not a full income statement.

## Deferred scope

PDF export, statutory reports, accounts payable/receivable, purchase accounting, cash sessions, tax filing, payroll accounting, and Phase 7 work remain deferred.

## Closure disposition

All items classified as needing Phase 6C integration were completed without a schema change. The canonical Dashboard, coverage/reconciliation API, Sale Finance trace, and Rust-owned CSV exports are active behind native capabilities. Fixture/demo surfaces remain isolated to browser preview, and every deferred item remains outside Phase 6.
